A diarization leaderboard buyers can trust

Amartya Singh · Voice Arena take-home, Q1 · one-page summary · full design in Amartya_Singh_Q1_Main.pdf · code & results: github.com/amartya1523/voice-arena-diarization-eval
The eval in one sentence: rank speaker diarization systems ("who spoke when") separately for meetings, phone calls, clinical consults, media and noisy field audio, on 60 hours of new hidden recordings, by DER scored the way products experience it (no collar, overlap counted, speaker count unknown), with confidence intervals and rank bands so a buyer never picks a "winner" that only won by chance.

Who it serves

  • Product teams picking an API/model for meeting notes, call analytics, scribes, subtitles.
  • Model builders who need to know where they fail (per condition, per error type).
  • Non-technical buyers who need a shortlist: "these two are tied, choose on price".

Core setup

  • Systems: 15 at launch across 6 types: open pipelines (pyannote community-1 / 3.1, 3D-Speaker), end-to-end open (NVIDIA Sortformer, DiariZen), diarization API (pyannoteAI Precision-2), STT APIs with speaker labels (Deepgram Nova-3, AssemblyAI, AWS Transcribe, Google Chirp 3, Azure, Speechmatics, ElevenLabs Scribe, OpenAI gpt-4o-transcribe-diarize), live and on-device.
  • Data: 60 h hidden test + 9 h public dev; 5 tracks; 430+ speakers; English accents + Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi; laptop, room array, PSTN/VoIP, speakerphone, car.
  • Reference: every speaker also recorded on a close-talk mic; forced alignment + human correction; 20% double-annotated; inter-annotator DER is the noise floor; "systems agree, label disagrees" audit.
  • Fairness: same file to all, language given, count not given, configs declared before testing, versions pinned and re-checked monthly, failures = 100% missed.

Metrics

  • Primary: DER = (missed + false alarm + speaker confusion) ÷ speech time, collar 0, overlap scored, system's own speech detection. Pooled per track; Overall = plain mean of tracks.
  • Beside it: DER parts, JER (each speaker counts equally), speaker-count accuracy, overlap DER, short-turn recall, WDER for STT systems, speed, $/hour, failure rate.
  • Winning for real: paired bootstrap over recordings; A beats B only if the 95% CI of the gap excludes 0 and the gap ≥ 0.5 pt (Holm-corrected) → rank bands like "1–2".

Most important design choices

ChoiceWhy
Per-use-case tracks, no single winner by defaultA system best on clean podcasts can be poor on 8 kHz calls; one number misleads most users.
Collar 0 + overlap scoredThe legacy setting hides short turns and crosstalk, the errors users notice. Legacy DER exported, never ranked.
Count unknown in the rankNo product knows who will join. Hinted results shown in a side column.
Rank bands, not ranksTies are real; fake precision drives bad purchases.
New hidden data, public sets only as anchorsAMI/VoxConverse are likely in training data.
